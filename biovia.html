Rethinking ONE Lab’s batch release workflow for scientists: from a heuristic evaluation to a redesigned and tested experience.

02

Heuristic evaluation

The project started with a heuristic evaluation of the existing ONE Lab batch release screens, documenting usability issues screen by screen against established heuristic principles.

03

Redesign based on heuristic principles

Using the evaluation and the flows, I redesigned the batch release experience around visibility of system status, recognition over recall and consistent patterns. The workflow tracker always shows where scientists are, side panels keep details close without losing context, and work is grouped the way it is actually done.

Full prototype walkthrough

04

Usability study

The redesigned prototype was then tested in a usability study. These attention heatmaps are a snippet of the results, showing where participants looked while completing tasks.

05

Presented at BIOVIA Live 2026

I presented this framework and the new ONE Lab batch release experience at BIOVIA Live 2026, alongside presales and the industry portfolio manager, sharing our early design direction with the BIOVIA community.
