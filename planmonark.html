PlanMonark

A usability evaluation (n=16) of PlanMonark’s survey creation platform, from editing a survey to publishing it.

Role
UX research and UI design
Team
4 researchers, HEC Montréal
Client
Patricia Girard, Présidente & CEO
Tools
Figma, UX Tweak
01

Mandate

PlanMonark helps organizations turn employee survey data into meaningful action for management. The evaluation assessed the usability of survey creation, focusing on learnability, effectiveness, efficiency and user control.

Can first-time users create and edit surveys with the current interface and information architecture, with minimal errors, enough guidance and no cognitive overload?

  • Can participants modify an existing question without confusion or error?
  • Can they create new elements efficiently?
  • Can they customize the survey to match their brand?
  • Is the action to publish clear and discoverable?
  • Is the information architecture easy to understand?
02

Method

16participants, 19 to 58 years old
6 / 10beginner and regular survey creators
60 minmoderated sessions with think-aloud
2information architectures compared

Each session combined behavioural measures (time on task, success rate) with self-reported ones (Customer Effort Score, CSAT, NASA-TLX), plus a semi-structured interview. Group differences were tested with Mann–Whitney–Wilcoxon and Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.

03

Results

12/16edited a question successfully
10/16created survey elements
0/16customized the survey branding
2/16found how to publish

Information architecture was the root usability issue. Competing panels made navigation unpredictable, and critical actions like publishing and customization lacked visibility. The platform still showed learnability potential, and prior survey-tool experience became a liability: regular users looked for familiar patterns and took longer.

“Looks easy to use if you actually know where to go, it doesn’t look hard, it’s just unfamiliar.” P11
“I don’t know how to publish, should I go in the general?” P4
“Changing the color thing, I couldn’t find that for the life of me.” P13

Testing a revised information architecture

We compared PlanMonark’s current IA with our proposed version, counterbalanced across participants. For adding conditional logic, success rose from 3/16 to 9/16 with the proposed structure (p = 0.0156).

04

Recommendations

High

Simplify the menus

Navigation labels in common language, arranged by predictable workflows.

High

Reduce competing options

Keep the primary left panel open by default so secondary panels do not compete for attention.

High

Make publishing obvious

A visible “Publish” action in more than one place, with a confirmation modal and a clear published status.

Medium

Group styling with live feedback

Group customization by area and highlight the affected element on hover.

Medium

Add titles, hover labels and tooltips

Guide beginners and reassure regular users that they are in the right place.
