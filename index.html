Hope & Cope Cancer Wellness Center

Helping patients and caregivers discover Hope & Cope’s free cancer support at the right time.

UX ResearchDesign
Scope

A 10-person HEC Montréal research team partnered with Hope & Cope to understand why patients and caregivers miss, or step away from, its free support.

Key insight

Support is far more likely to be used when it is introduced by the medical care team, at a moment when people feel ready for it.
