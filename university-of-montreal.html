UX ResearchDesign

University of Montreal

Making Université de Montréal’s new French language admission requirements easier to understand for every applicant.

Role
UX research and design
Partner
Bureau de projets technologiques, Université de Montréal
Context
Master’s project
Interface
French
01

What prompted the research

Recent changes to French language proficiency requirements. Starting in winter 2026, Université de Montréal revised its French admission requirement to better support students, especially applicants who did not complete their studies entirely in French.

02

Our goal

Make the French language requirements easier to understand.

The right requirement depended on where applicants studied, which institutions they attended and in which language. That logic lived in long blocks of conditional text, so applicants had to work out their own case.

03

Understanding the requirements

The redesign splits the experience into two tabs, so applicants can either read the rules or get an answer for their own situation.

1

Answer first

Applicants see the requirement that applies to them instead of reading every possible case.

2

Plain structure

Headings, lists and a clear status banner replace dense conditional paragraphs.

3

Next step and deadline

Accepted tests and the submission deadline appear right in the result.
