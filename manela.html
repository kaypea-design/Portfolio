Founding Designer

Manela

A SaaS platform that helps new parents understand government policy, workplace entitlements and what their finances will look like on leave.

Role
Founding designer: product design, UX research and front-end development
Product
SaaS parental leave platform for parents and employers
Tools
Figma, Supabase, Vercel
01

The problem

Isolation

Employees had no central place to find support, track their leave status or get answers to basic questions.

Hidden compliance complexity

Entitlements vary by location, employment type and tenure, and existing tools did not make this clear.

Opaque finances

Employees could not tell what they would actually be paid on leave, which made taking their full entitlement stressful.

02

Who it’s for

How might new parents understand the government application process, their workplace entitlements and the financial changes that come with a reduced income?

03

Design principles

A

Clarity over completeness

Show only what is needed, when it is needed, through progressive disclosure.

B

Trust through transparency

Financial and compliance figures are personal and based on the user’s own situation.

C

Guided, not gated

The experience should feel like a knowledgeable guide, not a bureaucratic form.

04

Scenario Planner: before and after

The shift: from a calculator that showed numbers to a guide that tells a story about your leave, organized into Timeline, Sharing leave and Breakdown.

05

A guided mobile flow

On mobile, planning became a step-by-step flow that asks about goals first, then shows the result.

06

Leave guides

Beyond the numbers, parents needed to know what to do and when. The guides turn leave into a calm, dated checklist.

07

Manela for companies

To reach parents through their employers, Manela also offers a branded hub for companies that brings their policy, top-ups, benefits and contacts into one place.

08
